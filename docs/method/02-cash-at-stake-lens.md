# Wave 3 search lens — cash at stake, not compliance cost

**Binding on all Wave 3 agents. Supplements, does not replace, `01-opportunity-scoring-rubric.md`.**

## Why the lens changed

Two shortlists died. Both died the same way: every candidate breached its own regulator's published
cost-benefit ceiling. UK regulators model ongoing compliance at **£70–£2,400 per firm per year**.
The UK buyer-count arithmetic needs **£50,000+ ACV**. That intersection is close to empty.

**A regulator caps what compliance is deemed to cost. Nobody caps what a business will pay to
recover its own money.** Wave 3 hunts the second thing.

## The six value mechanisms

Every Wave 3 candidate must sit in one of these, and must name the pool of money it touches.

| # | Mechanism | The question | Pricing basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Revenue leakage / income assurance** | Money owed to the customer that they never collect | % of recovered income |
| 2 | **Cost recovery / overpayment** | Money the customer paid that they should not have | % of recovery |
| 3 | **Working capital / cash timing** | Money trapped — late payment, retentions, disputes, credit | % of cash released |
| 4 | **Unclaimed entitlement** | Reliefs, allowances, grants, rebates the customer is eligible for and does not claim | % of claim |
| 5 | **Mispriced risk** | Premium, claims or credit priced on bad data | share of premium saved |
| 6 | **Yield / utilisation** | Capacity, stock or assets sold below their potential | % of uplift |

## The proof-of-value test — the strongest signal available

> **If firms already pay a contingent-fee consultant a percentage to do this manually, willingness
> to pay is proven and quantified.**

A recovery-audit firm taking 20–30% of what it finds is a live, priced market with no CBA ceiling
over it. Find those firms; their fee rate *is* the evidence. That is LAW 3 class-2 evidence of the
strongest kind — better than a job posting, because it is a price.

## Additional Wave 3 requirements

Every candidate must additionally answer:

1. **Name the money pool.** How much cash flows through this, annually, in the UK? Cite it.
2. **What fraction leaks?** Find the evidenced leakage/error/underclaim rate. This is the product's
   entire value proposition and must be sourced, not assumed.
3. **Who pays the consultant today, and what percentage?** Name firms and rates.
4. **Can the value be *proven* to the buyer?** A recovery product must demonstrate the money. If the
   saving is notional or contested, the sale collapses. Rate: PROVABLE / ARGUABLE / NOTIONAL.
   **NOTIONAL is an automatic rejection.**
5. **Is the data obtainable?** Recovery products need the customer's transactional data. Will they
   hand it over? What format? This killed candidates in Wave 2.
6. **Does it recur, or is it a one-off sweep?** A one-off recovery is a consulting engagement, not a
   SaaS. **The product must have a reason to run every month.** State it.

## Carried forward, unchanged

- **UK only.** **Cloudflare only.** Must survive to 2031–2036.
- **No sub-£10k ACV.** 47.7% of UK software companies ever incorporated are dissolved.
- **Must have an expansion axis** — per site, per unit, per transaction.
- **LAW 1c:** any regulatory claim requires the quoted provision from legislation.gov.uk.
- **LAW 4:** hunt the incumbent before falling in love. Contingent-fee consultants ARE incumbents.
- **"AI will do this" is a threat, not a feature.**

## Anti-pattern warning

Do **not** return "a dashboard for X" or "a compliance register for Y". Wave 3 is looking for
**money that is demonstrably being lost**, an evidenced leakage rate, and a buyer who will pay a
share of it back. If you cannot state the pounds, you do not have a candidate.
